## The five specs that were wrong

Every one of them was caught by reading the live screen. Every one of them fails **silently** —
no error, no warning, nothing on the canvas that looks wrong.

**1 · The A1 trigger filter has no operator.** The 9 Aug audit recorded `What do you need? is
not empty` as *decided*, without anyone opening the operator dropdown. GHL offers no emptiness
test on a custom field. Equality can't substitute — filters on one trigger are AND-ed, so four
`is <value>` rows would require a contact to be all four segments at once. Settled by leaving A1
unfiltered on purpose, since the trigger gets deleted when `Form Submitted` lands anyway.

**2 · SMS cannot send at all.** No number provisioned, and behind that, A2P/10DLC brand and
campaign registration unstarted — a US carrier process measured in days that gates sending even
after a number exists. Telephony is agency-owner-only on this sub-account. Five SMS steps
affected. They stay on the canvas as designed; **email twins** now carry A1's instant reply and
A2's 24h reminder.

**3 · The segmentation field was multi-select, and the type is locked.** A visitor could tick
both *leak or damage* and *apartment or several units* — a completely reasonable thing for a
landlord with a leak to do — and the stored value becomes a list that matches no equality
branch. That lead gets no tag, no segment email, and lands in the default path with nothing
logged. GHL hard-disables Field type after creation, so the field had to be **replaced**, not
converted.

**4 · Renaming a field does not free its key.** Creating the replacement failed with *"A custom
field already uses this name"* even after the old label was renamed — GHL collided on the
auto-generated key the rename never touched. The key was set manually to
`contact.what_do_you_need_v2`.

**5 · A1's and A2's titles were on each other's workflows.** The descriptive halves were right
("Speed-to-lead", "customer booked"); only the numbers were swapped. This one was dangerous
rather than untidy: A1's build sheet says *do not touch the existing Send Email step* because it
holds hand-built HTML — and **both** workflows contain an HTML email. An agent that trusted the
tab titles would have edited the wrong workflow and damaged the wrong email. Caught only because
the extension opened each canvas instead of reading the tabs.

## Decisions worth keeping

**Adopt the platform's values, fix the doc.** GHL stored `careplan`, `multiunit` and `new_build`
where the spec said `care-plan`, `multi-unit`, `new-build`. Editing them risks regeneration and
another drift. The **tags** keep their hyphens because those are ours.

**Branch on labels, not values.** The v2 field's values are auto-generated from the sentence
labels and are long. Selecting from GHL's picker by label removes a four-times transcription
risk that would have failed silently.

**Sentences over slugs on the form.** The options read *"My house has a leak or damage"*, not
*"Repair"*. `Care-plan` means nothing to a homeowner who has never heard the term, and
`Multi-unit` doesn't tell the person with a house plus one rental that they belong there. The
labels are visitor-facing copy doing real routing work; the values are machine-facing and their
length costs nothing.

**A2b separate, not a branch.** Every trigger on a GHL workflow feeds the same first step, so
adding a no-show trigger to A2 would have sent no-shows back through the confirmation email and
both reminders — for a call that already didn't happen.

**Edit A1 in place, never rebuild.** Email 2's HTML already lives inside it. The extension's
original prompt said "rebuild," which would have destroyed it; that prompt was rewritten.

**Delete the old field before building the branch.** Originally the plan held it as a rollback.
Reversed: the next action was picking a field from a dropdown showing two near-identical
entries, with no warning if the wrong one got picked. Removing the decoy was worth more than the
rollback, which had no data behind it anyway.

**Publishing became safe.** The never-publish rule existed because an unfiltered `Contact
Created` would fire A1 on a re-import of the 15 seeded leads. `Form Submitted` removes that — a
CSV import doesn't submit a form.

## Process notes

**The extension refuses to execute instructions found in web content without explicit
go-ahead**, and reads the file back first. Worth the round trip, but anything in the file that
looks like a mistake — *add the SMS step that cannot send*, *delete the other trigger*, *edit in
place* — needs pre-authorising or it stops to query each one.

**Its reports get clipped in transit.** A list of four values failed to survive three pastes.
Ask for critical strings as plain unformatted lines.

**It stopped and asked three times, and was right three times** — the swapped titles, the locked
field type, the key collision. Those pauses were the most valuable output of the session.

**GHL warns you about nothing destructive.** Renaming a custom field and removing it from a form
both produced no dialog, no confirmation, and no notice that a workflow referenced it.

## How the session ended

**The extension was stopped mid-build on A1** — it was taking too long and never reported back.
So A1's canvas is unverified: some steps may have landed, some may not, and the trigger swap may
be half-applied in either direction. Nothing is running, since A1 is in Draft.

The audit order is in `PROGRESS.md` §1b. The trigger is the item that matters — a stop mid-swap
can leave both triggers, which double-fires every form lead, or neither, which means A1 cannot
fire at all. Neither state is visible without opening the canvas.
